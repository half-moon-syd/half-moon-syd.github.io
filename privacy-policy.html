Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Half Moon Yemeni Restaurant ("we", "us", "our") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and respecting the personal data of our patrons and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This policy applies to all interactions with our website (https://half-moon-syd.github.io/), phone inquiries, table reservations, and food orders.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information necessary to deliver our services, including:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and email address when you make a phone reservation or catering inquiry.
  • Order Information: Delivery instructions and meal preferences submitted when you place an order with us.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through anonymous server logs to optimize our site's speed and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and handle personal data exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To confirm, prepare, and complete dining reservations and takeaway orders.
  • To answer inquiries and provide customer service.
  • To maintain the performance, accessibility, and security of our online platform.
  • To comply with Australian regulatory and public health obligations.

4. Third-Party Delivery Platforms & External Links

Our website provides direct links to trusted third-party delivery partners, including Uber Eats and DoorDash, as well as social media networks (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) and navigation mapping services (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze).

When ordering through third-party platforms, your transactions and personal data are governed by their respective privacy statements. We encourage you to inspect their privacy policies directly.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict operational and electronic safeguards to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing brokers.

6. Accessing & Correcting Your Information

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you and request that corrections be made. To request access or update your information, please contact us using the details below.
