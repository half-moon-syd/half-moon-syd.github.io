Our Culinary Roots

Authentic Yemeni Tradition in the Heart of Western Sydney

Yemeni cuisine is one of the world's oldest and most revered culinary traditions—rooted in the ancient spice routes that connected the Arabian Peninsula with the Indian Ocean.

At Half Moon, our kitchen honors these time-tested methods. From marinating our chicken in whole spices ground in-house to slow roasting over gentle heat, our food delivers the deep smoky notes and tender texture that Yemeni home cooking is famous for.

  • Ancient Spice Blends: Loomi (dried black lime), Yemeni hawaij, cardamom, and mountain cumin infused into every cut.
  • Pure Halal Integrity: Every ingredient, cut of meat, and sauce is 100% Halal certified for complete peace of mind.
  • Generous Arabian Hospitality: Generous portions meant for sharing with family, friends, and community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers regarding our halal certifications, dining options, parking, and traditional specialties.

Where is Half Moon Yemeni Restaurant located in Sydney?

Half Moon is located at Shop 1, 168-172 Merrylands Road, Merrylands NSW 2160 in Greater Western Sydney. We are positioned in the main retail strip just 200 meters from Merrylands Railway Station, with multiple free council car parks and street parking bays nearby.

Is all the food at Half Moon 100% Halal certified?

Yes, 100%. All meats, spices, dressings, and culinary preparations at Half Moon strictly follow Islamic Halal standards. We take pride in serving high-quality halal chicken and authentic traditional sides to our community.

What are Half Moon's signature Yemeni specialties?

Our signature highlights include:

  • Slow-Roasted Yemeni Spiced Chicken: Whole and half chicken roasted with traditional aromatic herbs.
  • Fragrant Basmati Rice: Seasoned with dried lime, whole cardamom, and caramelized onions.
  • Areekah (عريكة): Warm crushed date bread with pure honey and rich clotted cream.
  • Masoob (معصوب): Sweet mashed bananas and wholewheat bread topped with cream and honey.
  • Handcrafted Garlic Toum & Sahawiq: Fresh house dips for the ultimate flavor pairing.

  • Friday – Saturday: 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)
  • Sunday: 11:00 AM – 11:59 PM
Can I order delivery to my home or office in Sydney?

Yes! We partner with both Uber Eats and DoorDash for prompt, hot delivery right to your door across Western Sydney. You can also phone in your order directly on (02) 8071 5833 for fast takeaway collection.

Do you accept dine-in table reservations?

Yes! While walk-in diners are always warmly welcomed, we recommend giving us a quick call at (02) 8071 5833 if you plan on visiting during peak weekend dinner hours or wish to book for large family and group feasts.
